The Origin

Born In A Smudged Garage

Shaon's Moto Co. started with a single wrench set, a battered 1969 British twin, and a steadfast refusal to accept mass-produced sterility. Over five decades later, our ethos has remained unyielding: strip away the unnecessary, amplify the mechanical heartbeat, and build motorcycles with personality.

Every chassis welded, tank hand-hammered, and carburettor synchronized in our workshop is tuned to deliver an unmatched visceral roar that connects machine and highway.
